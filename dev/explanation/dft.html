Why a Fourier transform at all ​

For a signal sampled at rate with samples, bin is the frequency . A tone that is not exactly on a bin spreads over several bins. This is the leakage that windows trade off against resolution; the spectrum tutorial uses a record that contains whole cycles, so none occurs.

The bins above are the negative frequencies. For real input  , so the spectrum of a real signal carries only half its information, and rfft returns that half.

Multiplying spectra is convolution in time: the cyclic convolution of two sequences is the inverse transform of the product of their transforms. A linear convolution is the cyclic one of zero-padded inputs. This turns filtering of long signals into work; the convolution tutorial shows it.
