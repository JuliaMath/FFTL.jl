AbstractFFTs

FFTL implements the plan side of AbstractFFTs.jl. Which methods each plan type provides:

methodcomplex plansreal plans (rfft, brfft, irfft)real-to-real plans
AbstractFFTs.Plan supertype✓✓✓
size, ndims✓✓✓
AbstractFFTs.fftdims✓✓✓
mul!(y, p, x)✓✓✓
p * x✓✓✓
AbstractFFTs.plan_inv (inv(p), p \ y)✓ (ScaledPlan)✓✓

Real plans are RFFTLPlan (rfft) and BRFFTLPlan (brfft, irfft); the complex ones are FFTLPlan; real-to-real are R2RPlan.

What FFTL does not implement

  • AbstractFFTs.plan_fft and AbstractFFTs.fft for standard arrays. FFTL does not register itself as the backend of the generic functions, so AbstractFFTs.fft(x) needs another backend such as FFTW.jl loaded. The reason is in Using FFTL next to FFTW.jl.

Inverses

inv(p) of a forward complex plan is the normalised inverse, a ScaledPlan wrapping the backward plan. For a real-to-real plan, inv applies the partner kind and the normalisation of the kind; the factors are on Cosine, sine and Hartley transforms.
