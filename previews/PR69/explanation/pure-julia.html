Other pure-Julia FFTs ​

FFTL is not the only FFT written in Julia. The packages on this page have goals of their own and are not part of the Benchmarks, which compare FFTL with the full-featured reference libraries that it would replace as a dependency (FFTW, ducc0, vDSP, cuFFT, VkFFT).

FFTA.jl ​

FFTA.jl ("Fastest Fourier Transform in my Apartment") is built for genericity. It transforms arrays of any element type that provides a few basic operations, so the same call computes an FFT of Complex{BigFloat} numbers in high precision, of symbolic variables from Symbolics.jl (which shows the formula of a transform), or of a small SVector from StaticArrays.jl. Its code base is small and readable.

FFTJ.jl ​

FFTJ.jl is an experimental FFT built on SIMD.jl and the AbstractFFTs interface. Several of its ideas have been contributed to FFTL, among them the batched resampling of lengths with a smooth part and a large prime factor (see Lengths with a smooth part).

PureFFT.jl and PureFFTGPU.jl ​

PureFFT.jl started as an investigation of where the speed of FFTW and RustFFT comes from and whether pure Julia can reach it, and aims to become a general-purpose, dependency-free FFT library. It transforms complex data of any length, with Rader's and Bluestein's algorithms for primes, has real transforms, and offers autotuned plans. Its documentation explains in detail which technique contributes what to the speed.

PureFFTGPU.jl is its companion for GPUs with the same interface: kernels written in Julia with KernelAbstractions.jl, with vendor-specific kernels as package extensions, rather than a wrapper around the vendor library.
